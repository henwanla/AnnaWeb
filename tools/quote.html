Compare the scope,
then compare the cost.

Put two custom hand fan quotes on the same basis. Enter whole-order amounts, mark what is already included, and keep costs not yet quoted visible.

Fictional example only. These amounts are not Fanique prices, tax rates, freight estimates or supplier offers.

Are these amounts comparable?

A delivery-term label alone does not confirm which costs are included. Compare supplied amounts in one currency; this tool does not convert currencies or choose a supplier.

Count each cost once.

The quoted amount is the whole-order amount supplied by the seller. For each cost below, choose:

  • Not yet quoted: the amount or its scope is unknown.
  • Included: already covered by the quoted amount; add nothing again.
  • Confirmed extra: enter the additional whole-order amount.
  • Not applicable: confirmed to be outside this purchase.

Check before comparing.

  • Match model, dimensions, materials, print sides, artwork version and quantities per design.
  • Confirm delivery term, named place, handover point and which services each amount covers.
  • Keep sample, setup, import and optional costs explicit. Unknown does not mean zero.
  • Check quote validity and payment terms separately. A complete entered cost is not a delivery guarantee or a quality assessment.
Where the method comes from

The cost fields are a comparison worksheet. ICC explains Incoterms rules and their scope. This tool does not reproduce the full rules or determine tax, customs or carrier requirements.

Your entries stay in this browser tab. The CSV is generated locally. No amounts are sent to a server, and no email is needed.

CSV text that starts like a spreadsheet formula receives a leading tab. The tab remains in the exported data; check these text fields when importing into another system.

Prepared as a research sample. Production prices, delivery options and service availability must be confirmed for the actual order.
