SAME PIXELS, DIFFERENT SIZE

Silent visual explanation, 44 seconds.
Calculator: https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/resources/artwork-resolution/

00–06 seconds: One image. Different placed sizes. A fictional folding hand fan artwork example.

06–13 seconds: 1800 by 1200 pixels at 15 by 10 centimetres gives 304.8 PPI in both directions.

13–20 seconds: The same 1800 by 1200 pixels at 30 by 20 centimetres gives 152.4 PPI in both directions.

20–27 seconds: Double the placed width and height, without adding pixels: PPI halves in both directions.

27–35 seconds: PPI is not artwork approval. Match the image area, then confirm the model, template, print requirements and sample.

35–44 seconds: Use the calculator for your actual pixels and placed size. These examples are not fan dimensions or approval targets.

PPI = source pixels used / placed size in inches. One inch = 2.54 cm.
The same source image and image area are used in both placements.
No PPI threshold, model size, printable area or artwork approval is prescribed.
