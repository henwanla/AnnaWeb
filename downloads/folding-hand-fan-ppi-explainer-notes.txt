FOLDING HAND FAN ARTWORK: PPI EXPLANATION CARD

Card and method: https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/resources/artwork-resolution/

Example
The same fictional 1800 x 1200 pixel image at 15 x 10 cm gives 304.8 PPI in both directions. At 30 x 20 cm it gives 152.4 PPI. PPI = pixels / placed size in inches. The rectangles share a scale; their sizes are examples, not actual Fanique print areas, fan dimensions, templates or artwork approval thresholds. No particular PPI target is prescribed.

Use pixels and placed dimensions for the same image region. If the image has been cropped, the pixel dimensions must refer to that crop. Confirm the selected model's actual template, printable area and process requirements with the supplier.
