THE ARTWORK BECOMES AN OBJECT

Have a design in mind? Start with the version you want printed and the details you need to review in a sample.

A screen mock-up helps review words and placement. For a physical sample, ask whether it uses an existing design or your artwork revision. Compare the material, print and folding structure with the planned order, and agree which details you are approving.

Confirm availability, cost and what may differ in production. Keep the approved object and version with the design record.
