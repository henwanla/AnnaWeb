How is dimensional weight calculated?

Multiply the external length, width and height in centimeters, then divide by the applicable divisor in cm³/kg. The result is in kilograms.

Which divisor should I use?

It varies by carrier and service. DHL Express describes 5,000 as a typical divisor, but confirm the one that applies to your shipment.

Is this the amount I will pay?

No. Rates, rounding, minimums, routes and other charges are not calculated here. Use your carrier’s current rules and quotation.
