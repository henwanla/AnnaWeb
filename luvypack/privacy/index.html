Privacy

When you contact us

We use the email address and message you provide to reply about your packaging request. Any optional project details help explain that request. Please do not include payment information or sensitive personal documents.

Website form submissions are processed by Formspree. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, where its privacy terms apply. These contact services operate separately from website analytics.

Website analytics

Basic website analytics starts when you visit, unless you have turned it off in Analytics preferences. It helps us understand visits, packaging interests and contact actions. Your form message and email address are not sent as analytics event fields.

While analytics is on, this tab can remember a registered campaign label or recognized referral category and the page where it was observed. If you submit an email question, those labels accompany it to help us understand which content brought you here. We do not include search terms, full referring addresses or personal tracking identifiers. You can turn this off below.

While analytics is on, page and contact events are stored in our Cloudflare-hosted database and can be exported for internal analysis. Records include the page or packaging format, recognized source labels, approximate country and broad device category. We do not store raw IP addresses in this event database or use a visitor identifier for this collection. Hosting providers process connection information for website delivery and abuse prevention. Google Analytics also measures visits and may set analytics cookies while analytics is on. Google Signals and advertising features are disabled. Your saved off choice applies to Google Analytics and our site event records on later visits in this browser. Cloudflare Web Analytics may still collect browser performance information separately. You can change the setting at any time below.
