Our story

The detail
people remember.

The piece is chosen with care. The packaging should carry that same attention. Long before a ring is worn or a necklace becomes a familiar favorite, someone notices the surface, lifts the lid and sees what is inside.

LuvyPack is built around that first encounter. A good package gives the jewelry room to speak: a quiet exterior, an opening that feels natural, an insert that holds the piece in the right position. The details should work together, without asking the customer to think about them.

For a brand, those small decisions are also practical ones. Will a pendant sit comfortably beside its chain? Does the same finish work across a ring box and a longer necklace format? What changes when the package leaves a store counter and travels to someone’s home?

Our role is to bring those questions into the design conversation. Start with the piece, develop the structure, then connect the materials, logo and handover. Whether the direction is a structured box or a soft pouch, the aim is a package that feels like it belongs to the brand.

Our journey

Proposed company history · dates for review

  1. 2019

    A beginning in the details

    The story starts with a small packaging-sourcing desk and a close look at the things buyers notice first: how a lid sits, how a lining feels and how a logo meets the material. The early focus is jewelry presentation, one piece at a time.

  2. 2021

    From references to samples

    The focus moves beyond finding a box. Structure, artwork and sample feedback begin to be considered together, with closer coordination around the fit of the insert and the consistency of the finish.

  3. 2023

    One collection, more possibilities

    Boxes and soft pouches become part of the same conversation. The direction broadens from a single package to coordinated formats for rings, earrings, pendants and necklaces, each with its own interior needs.

  4. 2025

    Thinking beyond the box

    Attention turns to the complete handover: the reveal, the carrying layer and the outer delivery pack. The ambition is to help brands keep a recognizable visual language as their collections and sales channels grow.

  5. 2026

    A clearer way to begin

    LuvyPack brings its packaging directions into a browsable collection, supported by practical planning guides and a simple first conversation. The next chapter begins with the piece you want to package.

What we believe.
How it shapes the work.

A few clear principles, carried into practical decisions.

Start with the piece

Look at the jewelry before choosing the outer shape. Its proportions, contact points and removal path should guide the insert and the opening.

In practice: discuss the actual contents and dimensions before committing to a box size.

Let the brand feel consistent

A ring box, a pouch and a delivery pack have different jobs. Color, texture and logo placement can connect them without forcing every layer to look identical.

In practice: compare finishes together, including how the logo reads on each material.

Make each decision clear

A reference image is a starting point. The structure, artwork revision, sample scope and quoted inclusions need their own shared understanding.

In practice: keep the approved sample and specification connected as the project moves forward.

Make the first question easy

A project does not need to arrive as a finished specification. An idea, a photograph or one unresolved detail can be enough to begin a useful conversation.

In practice: start with a short email or WhatsApp message; build the fuller brief together.
