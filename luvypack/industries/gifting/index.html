Before you choose.

Should I choose a jewelry gift box or a ring pouch?

A structured box provides a defined opening and insert layout. A soft ring pouch offers a different handover and takes up less rigid space. Compare both with the actual piece and the way it will be given.

What should I check on a jewelry gift box sample?

Review the insert, lid clearance and removal of the piece. For a ribbon or drawer format, check that the closure and pull work smoothly with the intended contents.

Can I combine a box, pouch and brand card?

Discuss the complete set, dimensions and which items the quotation should include. Confirm that the packed arrangement works together; matching colors alone does not establish fit.
