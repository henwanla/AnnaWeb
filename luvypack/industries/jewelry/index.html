Ring packaging: support the band. Leave room for the setting.

Compare a PU-covered ring box, a paper box with a velvet lining and a ribbon-tied ring box. The holder, removal motion and closed-lid clearance matter as much as the exterior.

What to measure and check

Measure the band width and the highest point of the setting. Review how the ring sits in the slot and whether the lid clears the jewelry.

Earring packaging: allow space behind the presentation.

Studs, drops and hooks need different arrangements. Compare earring-box designs with the full pair, posts and backs in mind.

What to measure and check

Record the drop length, width, post projection and back size. On the sample, check the mounting position, space behind the pad and how the pair is removed.

Measurement checklist
  • Chestnut PU Earring Box
  • Graphite Lined Earring Box
  • White & Blue Ribbon Earring Box

Necklace & pendant packaging: give the chain a clear path.

Compare compact pendant boxes with longer necklace formats. Choose the layout around the pendant, chain and clasp rather than the chain length alone.

What to measure and check

Show the intended display arrangement and the space for the clasp. Check pendant movement, chain routing and removal with the actual piece.

Before you choose.

How do I choose a ring box, earring box or necklace box?

Start with the piece and its intended presentation. Rings need a suitable holder and setting clearance; earrings need room for posts and backs; necklaces need an agreed chain and clasp arrangement. Review the insert with the actual jewelry before approving the size.

Can the jewelry packaging carry my logo?

Discuss the logo position and finish on the chosen covering. Share the artwork or a reference, then confirm the placement and finish on the agreed sample.

Where can I compare the ordering details?

The packaging directory shows the collection minimums and production-time references. Open a design to compare its structure and finish, then confirm the size and artwork split in your quotation.
