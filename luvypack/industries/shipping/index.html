Before you choose.

Can a jewelry presentation box be used as the shipping box?

Treat the presentation and delivery as separate tasks. Review an outer mailer, the inner box or pouch and any packing material as a complete packed order. Agree the transport checks for the intended route.

How do I choose the mailer size for a jewelry order?

Measure the complete inner pack, cards and any wrapping or packing material. State the units and whether the requested box dimensions are internal or external. Confirm the final packed fit on a sample.

Does a soft pouch replace transport protection?

A pouch can hold the piece within the presentation, but that alone does not establish transport protection. Review movement, contact and the complete packing arrangement under the delivery conditions you plan to use.
