From the piece
to a clearer sample.

Structure, finish and sample scope are separate decisions. Work through them in order.

  1. Start with the piece

    A product photo, a rough size or a short question is enough to begin.

  2. Choose the format

    Compare the opening, insert and size around the actual piece and its use.

  3. Refine the brand detail

    Work from the selected structure when discussing color, print and logo placement.

  4. Agree the sample scope

    Confirm the design revision, sample checks, quotation and lead-time starting point together.

Fit first.
Finish with intention.

Review the contents, insert and closure before refining the artwork. A finish should support the design rather than hide a structural problem.
