What should a packed sample show?
  1. The whole set. Include the jewelry, inner box or pouch, cards and any wrapping.
  2. The space between layers. Check the remaining gaps with the chosen packing material in place.
  3. The handover. Review whether the contents stay in position and how the presentation looks when opened.
  4. Transport testing, if needed. Agree the delivery conditions, complete packed contents and acceptable condition of both the product and packaging before testing.

Check the packed fit and handling needs together. Explore branded delivery, or ask about your delivery project.
