Home/Planning/Compare packaging formats

Choose the opening before the finish.

A practical way to compare magnetic boxes, drawer boxes, mailers and retail cartons for your packaging project.

Start with the contents, handling and intended presentation. Then compare the structure and the opening.

FormatOpeningDiscuss first
Reveal Magnetic Gift BoxHinged lid with a magnetic front flapPlan the lid clearance and insert around the actual contents. Review the closed box as well as the first opening.
Ribbon-Pull Drawer BoxSliding tray inside an outer sleeveCheck drawer clearance with the finished wrap and the intended contents. The ribbon pull and finger access should work together.
Two-Piece Presentation BoxSeparate lift-off lidDecide how much of the base should show and how the lid lifts. Test the fit using the chosen paper wrap.
Flat-Pack Magnetic BoxFold-up body with a hinged front flapReview corner assembly and the flat-packed dimensions. Freight implications depend on the actual packed quantity and shipping arrangement.
Branded MailerConnected lid with tuck-front closureChoose the board and fit around the packed product, then agree any transit checks needed for the actual shipping route.
Folding Retail CartonTuck-end openingConfirm the dieline before placing artwork. Check flap engagement and the assembled fit with the actual product.
Paper TubeLift-off capCheck the internal diameter, usable height and cap fit. Any direct contact with contents needs its own material review.
Modular Paper Insert SetRemovable insert within an outer packUse product dimensions and weight to discuss the fit. Review removal notches and any checks needed for the intended handling.
Retail Paper BagOpen top with ribbon handlesSize the bag for the packed box rather than the product alone. Confirm handle construction and the intended carrying load.
Jewelry Presentation BoxHinged display lidReview the piece dimensions, insert contact points and display angle. Material suitability depends on the actual jewelry and finish.
Brand Launch KitPresentation box with a fitted multi-item trayProvide the dimensions and weight of every included item, then review the complete arrangement together.
Countdown Drawer BoxIndividual pull-out drawersStart with the number, dimensions and weight of the contents. Review drawer clearance and the overall packed size.
