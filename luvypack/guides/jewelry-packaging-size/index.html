Jewelry packaging sizes: measure the piece, plan the fit.

Learn what to measure for ring, earring and necklace packaging. Compare item, insert and box dimensions, nominal pouch sizes and the complete packed order.

The jewelry, insert and outside pack have different dimensions. Record them separately so the quotation and sample refer to the same arrangement.

What to measure for each piece.

Use millimeters or inches consistently. Photograph the jewelry beside a ruler and note the layout you want to show when the box opens.

Item size is not the box size.

  • Jewelry dimensions: the piece and its proposed arrangement, including posts, chain and clasp.
  • Usable insert space: where the jewelry sits, the support around it and the room needed for removal.
  • Internal box dimensions: the agreed cavity and clearance with the insert fitted.
  • External box dimensions: the outside of the finished pack, including the construction and covering.

Ask the quotation to identify which dimensions are being used. Agree clearance around the actual piece and insert rather than adding a universal allowance. Jewelry-box dimensions are confirmed for the selected project.

Measure the complete order for the mailer.

  1. Assemble the intended inner box or pouch, jewelry, card, wrapping and packing material.
  2. Record the full packed footprint, height and weight. Note what can move or touch another surface.
  3. Specify the proposed mailer dimensions, units and whether they are internal or external.
  4. Review packed fit, closure and handling with a sample. Agree any transport testing for the destination and route.
