A clearer brief starts with a few useful details.

Prepare the product dimensions, quantities, artwork and sample questions that help shape a packaging quotation.

You can ask a first question without a finished brief. When you are ready to compare a quotation, gather the details below.

  • What goes inside: a photo, dimensions and weight.
  • The packaging format and intended use.
  • A rough quantity and any planned variants.
  • Print areas, artwork and finish ideas.
  • Insert, card and any secondary packaging.
  • Sample scope and what needs approval.
  • Delivery destination and the timing you want to discuss.
