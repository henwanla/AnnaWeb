Will it fit inside the box?

Compare the overall size of an item with a box's usable inner space. Add a little room on every side, then check each possible orientation.

Check the dimensions

1. Item: overall outer size

Include protruding parts, backs, clasps or any fixed holder.

2. Box: usable inner space

Measure the space available after any insert or lining is in place.

For example, 2 mm per side adds 4 mm to each dimension. You choose the clearance; there is no universal allowance.

Before you measure

Piece, insert, box.

  1. Measure the full item as you intend to place it, including any attachment.
  2. Measure the usable cavity with its insert fitted, not the outside of the box.
  3. Check the opening, removal, lid clearance and contact points with a physical sample.

A dimensional match is a starting point, not proof of a safe fit or shipping protection. Rigid-envelope calculations cannot account for shape, materials or movement.

Inside or outside?

A finished box's outside dimensions include its walls and covering. Compare the item with the usable inside, after the insert is in place.

Can the item rotate?

The tool checks all six axis alignments. A listed alignment may still be unsuitable for a chain, a fragile setting, a shaped insert or the desired presentation.

What should I send?

Share the item's size, a photo, the preferred packaging and approximate quantity. Clarify units and whether a requested box size is internal or external.

Frequently asked questions

How many millimeters are in an inch?

One international inch is exactly 25.4 mm. The unit selector converts the values already entered; displayed values are rounded to six decimal places. NIST length reference.

Does a fit result mean I can ship the item safely?

No. This tool compares rectangular measurements only. Test the complete packed order, including cushioning and closure, for your delivery conditions.

Is the calculation sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in this page in your browser. The tool does not submit your measurements or create an inquiry.
