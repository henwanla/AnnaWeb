Use only what your project needs

  1. Save a copy. Record the current artwork or requirement version so later changes can be distinguished.
  2. Separate the details. Keep total quantity and quantity per design distinct. Record frame and fabric separately, and explain the measuring endpoints for dimensions.
  3. Leave confirmations open. Buyer notes do not establish material specifications, availability, sample scope, minimum quantities or delivery commitments.
  4. Add timing when relevant. Keep the usable-by date, event date and buffer separate. Leave unknown dates and durations blank until there is a basis to plan them.

Check image pixels at the placed artwork size

Preparing a raster image for a folding fan design? Use the folding hand fan artwork resolution calculator to calculate effective PPI in both directions from your image pixels and placed dimensions. No file upload is required; this is a density calculation, not artwork approval.

Check an item against a box

Planning a box for a product or gift? Use the packaging fit calculator to compare an item’s dimensions with usable inner space and a clearance you choose. The result is a first measurement check; confirm the insert and opening with a sample.

After receiving quotes: record the cost scope

Use this separate blank worksheet to record a quotation's total and clarify transport, import, final-delivery and agreed extra charges. It contains no supplier prices, calculated totals or shipping promises.

Prefer to work on screen? Compare two hand fan quotes in your browser. Enter the supplied whole-order amounts, record which costs are included or separate, and keep unknown costs unresolved. The optional calculator starts blank, applies no currency conversion and keeps entries in the tab; it does not choose a supplier.

CSV · 378 bytes · Separate download; the project and delivery workbook and ZIP stay as listed above.

  1. Compare the same requirement. Check the format, material, artwork revision, quantity per design, currency and receiving point before comparing amounts.
  2. Record the quoted total once. Use quoted for the whole-order amount; use included for a cost already in that total, extra for a separately charged item, and na only when confirmed not applicable. Keep unknown until the scope is clear.
  3. Keep evidence with the amount. Record the quotation reference and confirmation date. A blank or unknown amount is not zero; included components must not be added to the quoted total again. This CSV does not calculate a payable or delivered cost.
