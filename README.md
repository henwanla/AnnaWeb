## About the design comparison

Informational website redesign prototype for reviewing the current Fanique page against a more coherent product and buyer-planning layout.

The concepts contain six published product formats, scenario entrances, local catalogue filtering, a product-aware project outline, four planning guides, two local calculators and empty CSV worksheets. It never automatically sends enquiries and processes no orders or payments. Direct WhatsApp links open the existing Fanique contact with product, occasion and page context; the visitor reviews and sends the message in WhatsApp. The optional local outline can also be opened in WhatsApp, including details the visitor entered. There are no analytics or external font requests. No new paid service has been added; the enquiry forms reuse the original website’s existing service. Design-comparison and calculator HTML pages ask search robots not to index the previews. The separate `resources/` pages are indexable documentation for the existing blank worksheets and artwork pixel-density calculation. Download files have their own responses and unknown actual indexing states.

Product images are optimized derivatives of images published on the current Fanique website and are used for this user-requested redesign comparison. Product-family labels are not independently verified manufacturing specifications. No prices, minimum quantities or lead times are invented. The visual mark is a concept for this preview. The dark clack fan cutout is a background-removal derivative; the bamboo and PVC photos remain intact. Proposed use directions are concepts, not customer case studies.

The calculators preserve the existing locally reviewed calculation/export logic. They support planning rather than approving artwork or proving delivered cost. There has been no new browser visual or device-level test of this published page; the validation record describes the checks actually performed.

Open `index.html` for the Chinese style comparison portal. `night.html`, `atelier.html` and `pop.html` are three complete English page concepts with distinct hero compositions and browsing sequences. Portal thumbnails are style compositions rather than HTML screenshots. GitHub Pages is used to publish this informational comparison prototype; it does not replace the production website at getfanique.com.

## Match the pixel count to the area measured

The artwork guide now explains the conditional case of an image clipped without resampling or a change of scale. The full source-image pixel count must not be divided by a smaller clipped frame width. A fictional 1800 px / 30 cm example and its retained 1200 px / 20 cm portion both produce 152.4 PPI; mixing 1800 px with 20 cm would overstate the result as 228.6 PPI. These are mathematical examples, not actual fan dimensions or print requirements.

The hosted widget carries the same input warning in its calculation-method disclosure.
