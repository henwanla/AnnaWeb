# Fanique design comparison

Informational website redesign prototype for reviewing the current Fanique page against a more coherent product and buyer-planning layout.

The page contains six product-family cards, local filtering, a product-aware project outline, four planning guides, two local calculators and empty CSV worksheets. It never automatically sends enquiries and processes no orders or payments. Direct WhatsApp links open the existing Fanique contact with product, occasion and page context; the visitor reviews and sends the message in WhatsApp. The optional local outline can also be opened in WhatsApp, including details the visitor entered. There are no analytics, external font requests or paid services. Search robots are asked not to index this comparison site.

Product images are optimized derivatives of images published on the current Fanique website and are used for this user-requested redesign comparison. Product-family labels are not independently verified manufacturing specifications. No prices, minimum quantities or lead times are invented. The visual mark is a concept for this preview. The dark clack fan cutout is a background-removal derivative; the bamboo and PVC photos remain intact. Proposed use directions are concepts, not customer case studies.

The calculators preserve the existing locally reviewed calculation/export logic. They support planning rather than approving artwork or proving delivered cost. There has been no new browser visual or device-level test of this published page; the validation record describes the checks actually performed.

Open `index.html` for the Chinese style comparison portal. `night.html`, `atelier.html` and `pop.html` are three complete English page concepts with distinct hero compositions and browsing sequences. The previous full design is preserved as `classic.html`; `comparison.html` explains the page changes in Chinese. Portal thumbnails are style compositions rather than HTML screenshots. GitHub Pages is used to publish this informational comparison prototype; it does not replace the production website at getfanique.com.

Expected Pages address: https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/

Deployment status and the verified public address are recorded in the user’s Obsidian report after publishing.


The revised concepts use independent page architectures: an event-led experience (`night.html`), an editorial catalogue (`atelier.html`) and a procurement workspace (`pop.html`). They retain the original six scenario directions, six product descriptions, company positioning, factory introduction, two factory images, and existing supply notes with confirmation boundaries. Factory figures, nominal specifications and customer-like scenario images are current-site claims/illustrations, not new verified evidence. No fictional founder, client case, testimonial, certification or result is introduced. `core.css` shares basic controls; each page has its own layout stylesheet. Occasion choices carry into the local brief.


Contact revision: all four full concepts retain the existing WhatsApp number in the navigation, hero, each of six products, project area and footer. Each product has a distinct prefilled message and a no-JavaScript fallback. The three new concepts also have a factory-area WhatsApp link with a specific production, artwork/sample or supplier/inspection question. Its topic stays local to that link while the selected product, occasion and public page context update. Switching product or occasion updates the context; editing the outline hides the stale prepared output. Exact-date inputs have been removed from initial enquiries. Buyers may voluntarily mention an event deadline in optional details. Required fields are explicitly labeled for the optional outline; starting a direct conversation requires no form.

Each of the six occasion cards in the three revised concepts keeps a local planning link and adds its own direct WhatsApp link. The message fixes that card's occasion and discussion topic, even if another occasion was previously selected; a deliberately selected product and the public source page are also included. The fixed occasion, topic and public source are preserved without JavaScript. Neither path sends a message automatically.

The project-brief CSV is an optional follow-up quote record. It does not gate direct WhatsApp or the original-site contact form. Delivery deadlines and venue rules are recorded only when relevant; no first-contact date picker is introduced.
