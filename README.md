### Fifth and sixth concepts

The [reference and design rationale](https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/reference-rationale.html) documents the observed Richpack and Fanxi design principles, distinct page structures, marketing hypotheses and grill-me self-review. Competitor photos, copy, customers and credentials are not reused. Both concepts use existing Fanique assets and preserve the original six formats and six occasion directions, company and supply notes, and two factory images. Stock occasion photographs are mood references, not completed customer projects.

Both add fixed Email / WhatsApp contact, retain the original Formspree POST route, and require only email and question. Product email actions add a visible optional topic; input is not overwritten. WhatsApp uses fixed product/scene topics and the matching public page address, never form drafts or visitor query parameters. Native disclosures work without JavaScript; direct format links open the relevant details when the enhancement loads. Collection navigation keeps the overview closed rather than choosing the first format. Email navigation preserves the visitor's choice to keep optional project fields closed or expanded, and does not move focus past the primary email and question fields.

Local validation covers saved HTML/CSS, asset dimensions, paths, original-content preservation and in-memory interactions. The single-use UI permission covered viewing Fanxi only; actual new-page browser rendering, mobile behavior and real business receipt have not been verified. Old four concept files and shared enquiry behavior are retained unchanged.

### Current fourth concept

Six original SKU images lead the gallery and wide image-first product details. The occasion room uses six separate panels cropped from the user-supplied concept image: Weddings, Festivals, Sports, Corporate, Parties and Promotions. Titles are HTML outside the images. Desktop shows six vertical panels, tablet allows native horizontal browsing, and mobile uses two columns. Charities and Schools remain available through additional links, preserving all six original occasion routes and making eight occasions in total.

The two original factory photographs retain an offset documentary layout. SKU and occasion details combine an image with explanatory copy; About and Email use a paper-white side reading panel, or a bottom panel on narrow screens. The old blurred backdrop is removed. Fan-format changes use a restrained pivot motion, with button/keyboard alternatives and reduced-motion support.

The supplied occasion panels are concept illustrations, not verified customer work. Each crop is only about 250 pixels wide; high-resolution detail still needs independent source images. Charities and Schools use credited stock mood references. Original website illustrations remain accessible for the original six occasions. No artificial product back view, close-up, opening video or manufacturing evidence is invented.

These are topics to confirm, not new inspection certificates, refund/remake terms or delivery promises. No real test enquiry has been sent. - [Procurement workbook (Excel)](https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/downloads/fanique-procurement-worksheets.xlsx): Read me, Project brief and Delivery plan, with source and contact links. Original CSV files remain available through the guide.
- [Fixed planning-resource release](https://github.com/henwanla/AnnaWeb/releases/tag/planning-resources-2026-10-14): the original ZIP and archive digest, retaining its packaging date. A download count does not identify an independent buyer.
- [Blank quote cost-scope CSV](https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/downloads/quote-costs.csv) and [quote-scope explanation](https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/resources/#quote-scope-heading): record quoted, included, separate, unknown and not-applicable scope. Unknown is not zero, and included costs must not be added twice. No supplier prices or calculated totals are provided.

[Custom hand fan quote comparison calculator](https://henwanla.github.io/AnnaWeb/tools/quote.html): start with blank entries, compare confirmed amounts only after matching scope, and export a local CSV. The fictional example is loaded only on request; no seller prices are supplied.

The resource guide links to this quote calculator and back to the official website or a fixed WhatsApp planning question. That question contains the public resource/tool address, not completed cells or entered amounts. Candidate product/method layouts, four planning guides, local calculators and worksheets remain planning aids, with their stated confirmation limits.
