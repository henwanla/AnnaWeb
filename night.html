When discussing samples

Try the format.
Then confirm the design.

Start with what you need to decide for your occasion.

  1. How will it be used?

    Ask about a sample of the selected format to review handling, folding and distribution for your event.

  2. Which design will it show?

    Confirm whether the proposed sample is blank, uses an existing design, or carries your own artwork revision. A screen mock-up helps review words and placement.

  3. What are you approving?

    Agree which material, print and structure details the sample covers, and what may differ in the planned order. Confirm sample availability, costs and shipping before arranging it.
